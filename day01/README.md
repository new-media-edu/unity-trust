# Day 01: Foundations & Spatial Sketching

**Session Time:** 2.5 Hours

Welcome to your first steps in Unity. Today, we're moving from a blank slate to a walkable 3D gallery. We'll focus on the interface, basic physics, and building custom architecture.

##  Download Class Files

## The Modules

Work through these in order. Each one builds on the scene you made in the last.

### 1. [The Unity Interface](interface.md)
Setting up a URP project, the five windows you actually need, making your first objects, and getting around the 3D view. The navigation shortcuts here are the ones you'll use every single session, so it's worth getting them into your hands early.

### 2. [Physics in Action](physics.md)
Drop a Rigidbody on a sphere and watch gravity do the work. Short, and the fastest way to see that this is a live simulation and not a drawing.

### 3. [Architectural Sketching with ProBuilder](probuilder.md)
Installing ProBuilder and greyboxing a room: walls, stairs, pedestals. Extruding, insetting, and cutting holes in geometry. This is where your space actually gets built.

### 4. [Walking Through the Space](first-person.md)
Adding the first-person controller from the class files so you can walk around what you made.

*Do this one even if you're running behind. Seeing your room at eye height changes what you want to build next.*

## Homework

Expand your working 3D environment. Try modeling a building in your neighborhood or a famous piece of architecture.
